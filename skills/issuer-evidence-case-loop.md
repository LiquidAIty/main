# Issuer Evidence Case

@skill id=issuer-evidence-case-loop
@type Skill
@status active
@related_to knowgraph

Use when the actual user asks for evidence about a company or instrument. Recovered from
`2ddadeeb^` and refreshed September 9, 2026. The old case assembler and injected seed adapters
are not current runtime dependencies. This is an evidence research procedure, not a trading
agent configuration or a requirement to create a case/report node.

1. Resolve the issuer against actual company/exchange/filing sources or native graph identity.
   Distinguish ticker, issuer and instrument; report unresolved identity rather than guessing.
2. Answer the specific research question with relevant primary evidence. Business, customers,
   cash flow, ownership and risk are possible lenses, not a mandatory dossier for every query.
3. Separate persistent identity, source-backed assertions, dated observations, and your derived
   conclusions. Preserve source URL/reference, publication/filing date, reporting period and
   retrieval date when available. Do not invent missing dates or measures.
4. Inspect relevant existing native records and their provenance before treating them as usable
   context. Freshness and contradiction are research questions, not a reason to discard history.
5. Retain useful sourced findings through the existing granted research intake when requested.
   Keep user interest and Main's evolving interpretation in ThinkGraph, clearly attributed.

Market availability errors mean unknown availability, not evidence that an instrument cannot
be traded. User-supplied seed material is supplemental and unverified until its claims are
checked; it cannot overwrite primary evidence. Do not scan personal folders for seed files.

Avoid ticker-specific code branches, deterministic investment rankings, or fabricated evidence
objects. Research does not itself authorize orders, monitoring, a journal, or a later agent job.
Use this skill only for the relevant research task; it does not belong in every Main prompt.
