# Catalyst Research

@skill id=catalyst-research-case
@type Skill
@status active
@related_to issuer-evidence-case-loop

Use when a user asks how a specific reported event could affect an issuer. Recovered from
`2ddadeeb^` and refreshed September 9, 2026. Preserve the evidence discipline from the old
case experiment, not its deleted assembler, fixed schema, seed configuration or smoke commands.

Resolve the entity and event, inspect the primary source, and explain the possible mechanism
and uncertainty. A filing, contract award, forecast and market reaction are different kinds
of evidence. Do not promote a headline, optional contract amount, or model forecast into an
established economic result. Retrieve only the surrounding issuer context needed to assess it.

Keep dates separate: publication/filing date, event date, reporting period and retrieval date.
A 10-K/10-Q may describe a reporting period; an 8-K event date is not automatically a fiscal
period end. Do not infer dates from a ticker, accession number, filename or current time.
Retain exact source-supported values and units. Explain gaps in ordinary prose, without
inventing a new required gap schema, classifier or a checklist node in the graph.

Distinguish source observations, verified background, tentative supplemental material, and
derived interpretation. Apply [issuer-evidence-case-loop.md](issuer-evidence-case-loop.md)
only for the relevant issuer context. Use the existing KnowGraph intake for authorized sourced
findings; no automatic seed importer, extra writer or new graph authority.

The former experiment's package-import, seed-atlas and sec-api commands are not current setup
instructions. Inspect the actual source/response when diagnosing those boundaries. Test data
may reproduce a parsing defect in isolation; it must not become trusted product evidence.
